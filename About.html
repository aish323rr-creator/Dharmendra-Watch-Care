OUR STORY

A Passion That Stood
the Test of Time.

Dharmendra Watch Care has its roots in a family tradition of watch and clock repair. The shop was originally owned by Dharmendra's father, introducing the craft into the family long before it became Dharmendra's profession.

At just ten years of age, Dharmendra began working with clock repairs- initially driven by curiosity and a genuine interest in understanding how timepieces worked. What began as a childhood hobby gradually grew into a lifelong profession.

Today, with approximately 45-50 years of experience in the craft, he brings decades of hands-on knowledge to the repair of watches, clocks and digital timepieces.

For him, what started with curiosity became a skill refined over a lifetime.
